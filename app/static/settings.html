Default player

The player whose moves you want to see. Moves, Practice and the dashboard show only this player's decisions by default. You can still switch players on each page, and the app remembers what you pick there.

Player namePicked from the matches you imported

Error grading

How much equity a decision has to lose to count as an error. Changes save by themselves and regrade every match straight away; nothing is re-analysed.

Analysis engine

GNU Backgammon analyses every checker play and cube decision. Its 2-ply ("World class") matches XG's 3-ply. Changes save by themselves and apply to new imports and re-analysis.

DepthDeeper is stronger and slower

New .mat files in these folders are imported and analysed on their own. The app can read your files but never changes them.
